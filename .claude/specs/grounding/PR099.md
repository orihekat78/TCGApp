## DSL mapping

- a1: on-enter, set own deck top card face-down on self, then grant `突撃[キャラ]` through turn end.
- a2: on-scene declared, turn limit 1. Grant self AP+1000 through turn end.
- The optional `declareName` domain contains only registered character-card name components.
- A supplied name is written through `nameOverride`, replacing the printed name until turn cleanup.

## Q&A constraints

- Declining the optional name declaration keeps the AP+1000 change.
- A selected name identifies a character-card name; card number and printing are irrelevant.
- The UI and engine canonicalize an exact or uniquely identifying abbreviation to a registered candidate.
- A unique one-character error is accepted conservatively; ambiguous inputs such as `毛利` or `服部` are rejected.
- Event names and unregistered names are rejected before count, log, event, cost, or queue mutation.
