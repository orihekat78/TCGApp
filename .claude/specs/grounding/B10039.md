# B10039 白馬探 grounding

## Source

- Primary: `.claude/specs/cards-data/ct-p10/character.tsv`, rows `B10039` and `B10039P`.
- Printed metadata: No. 1099; white; Lv.4; AP 3000; LP 1; traits `探偵|高校生`.
- Printings: `B10039` R / `1783904137988913.jpg`; `B10039P` RP / `1783904137995147.jpg`.

## Ability mapping

1. `【解決編】【自分ターン中】` uses active on-scene continuous restriction
   `contactLeaveSelfTrigger`. It applies only to the opposing character's
   own `leave:to-remove` ability when removal cause is `contact-ap`.
   Q&A excludes removals caused by abilities/effects during contact.
2. `【事件緑＆白】【登場時】` uses `caseColor` with both colors, then looks at
   up to four cards in a private window. The controller may take zero or one
   green/white card, discards one card only after taking one, and orders all
   remaining looked cards at deck bottom.
3. `【ヒラメキ】` is optional `evidence:remove-by-action` and draws one card.

## Q&A decisions

- A deck with fewer than four cards is fully looked at.
- Looked cards remain in deck during the window; refresh occurs only when a
  selected card actually leaves deck. Any unselected looked cards then move
  to deck bottom.
- A qualifying card may be declined.
