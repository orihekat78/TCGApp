# B10099 / B10099P 赤井秀一＆安室透

- Source: official CT-P10 TSV and official Q&A; `npm run ground -- B10099 B10099P`.
- Printings: MR `1783904232432220.jpg`; MRP `1783904232439228.jpg`. Rules text is identical.
- Printed data: red/yellow, level 9, AP 8000, LP 2; traits Detective, FBI, Akai family, Café Poirot.
- During its controller's turn, the MR reduces every opposing scene character's effective level by 1. The continuous ability remains active from partner area.
- On its own entry, it may remove one character at level 7 or lower.
- During its controller's turn, up to twice per turn, each own entrant whose printed abilities are only Cut-in and/or Hirameki may be slept. If that succeeds, remove up to one character at or below the entrant's effective level. The trigger also works in partner area.
- Q&A: an entrant already asleep, or declining the optional sleep, still consumes one of the two trigger uses. Simultaneous entrants are separate triggers. The entrant and removal target are read at resolution; if the entrant left, the effect fails closed.
- Cut-in: during the controller's turn, AP +2000.
- Engine mapping: the enter listener saves live `$triggerChar` candidates into both gate and queued bindings. `levelMaxBound` rehydrates that candidate at resolution, retaining effective level changes and refusing stale UIDs.
- Rules: `09-cutin-disguise.md`, `15-abilities-effects.md`, `17-icons.md`, `18-mr.md`.
