# B04030 grounding

- Source: `character.tsv`; same text and behavior verified for `B04030P`.
- A1: action-end trigger looks at up to four own deck cards privately; choose up
  to one level-8-or-less character named `怪盗キッド` or source-name `黒羽快斗`.
- A1: a declined confirmation leaves every looked card to bottom. A zero-candidate
  private look still surfaces the standard empty picker confirmation before deck-bottom handling;
  an accepted card may enter from the deck or enter hand. Entering removes the
  original source.
- A1: `target.query.area:'deck'` consumes the entered card; `from:'deck'` alone
  would duplicate it.
- Evidence: `rules/07-action-flow.md`, `rules/15-abilities-effects.md`,
  `rules/22-qa-action-contact.md`; `tests/cards/official-qa/B04030.test.ts`.

## Horizontal

- `B04030P` is an exact behavior variant and uses the same descriptor.
- At a full human scene, the existing switch picker is reused for the enter
  choice; the chosen victim is passed to `sceneEnter`.
- `sceneRemove` is conditional on the successful `sceneEnter` bind, so a
  declined switch never removes the source. A zero-match private look retains
  the standard empty human picker confirmation before deck-bottom resolution.
