# B10087 grounding

- Source: CT-P10 character.tsv and official Q&A inspected 2026-07-26.
- Entry effect optionally removes exactly three deck cards. Three matching black cut-in cards create a contact with this character as attacker.
- Uses the gated mill binding so the match is limited to this effect. A deck of two or fewer cannot use the optional effect; no refresh occurs.
- The chosen opposing character may be active. Declining the one-card choice or a stale target creates no contact. The generated contact permits cut-ins and disguises but no guard.
- The contact draw trigger applies to any participation role, including being targeted or guarding, but only after the used cut-in resolves. Same-name nonparticipants do not qualify.
