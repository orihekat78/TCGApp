# B10046

- Source: CT-P10 character TSV and its official Q&A.
- Text: own turn-end; resolve only if own scene has card name `怪盗キッド`; choose up to one own-remove Big Jewel event, then choose partner area or hand.
- Timing: Kaito is checked at resolution. A Kaito entering after trigger allows resolution; leaving before resolution prevents it. The source may leave after triggering and its effect still resolves.
- Mapping: turn-end hook → resolution `sceneHas`; `bindPick` preserves one remove occurrence; `choice` sends that same bound index to `toPartnerArea` or `handAddFromRemove`.
- Edge cases: no Kaito, source left, zero selection, duplicate event IDs, both destinations, and AI/human paths are covered by `tests/cards/ct-p10/b10046.test.ts`.
