## Expected

When a human player owns two or more unresolved effects, that owner chooses their order regardless of activation order. Turn-player effects retain priority over non-turn-player effects. One eligible effect remains automatic.

## Actual

`dispatchEngineAction` always called `runAllUntilEmpty` after an action. Same-owner entries were consumed synchronously before `EffectStackPanel` could be shown or `setEffectOrder` could be dispatched.

## RCA

The stack had `ownerChosenOrder` and an unmounted reorder panel, but no resolver/UI boundary for the mandatory owner decision. The first implementation incorrectly limited that decision to one emission batch; official rules apply it to all unresolved effects owned by the priority player.

## Phase 3.5 implementation status

- Confirming an exact owner snapshot now renumbers every group entry to its submitted canonical index, including partial-order legacy saves. The mixed-owner/cross-batch/timestamp-divergence regression records the actual resolver execution sequence through `effect:resolve:start`.

- `effectTriggerBatchSeq` is state-owned, serializable, monotonic across save/reload, and recovers legacy saves from persisted entries. Nested emits restore the outer batch context.
- Every `event.queue` reached from a listener inherits that batch. Triggered entries retain `abilityId` and description for a distinguishable order UI.
- Human-owned unresolved entries stop the resolver across emission batches, including legacy unbatched saves. Dispatch authorization accepts only the current priority human's eligible unresolved group.
- A newly emitted timing clears the resolving parent's confirmation while its listeners run, then restores it. Direct continuation entries retain the confirmed parent batch, but nested simultaneous triggers require their own owner confirmation.
- The panel receives every eligible unresolved effect for the priority human and lives in a non-zoom fixed overlay. Desktop and `851x393` Playwright verify cross-batch visibility, viewport containment, 44px controls, reorder, and confirm.
- CPU stepping treats owner ordering as a human decision and cannot take another move or end the turn while it is pending. Confirming during the CPU turn bumps the driver wake-up tick so play resumes after resolution.
- Deferred target resolution reconstructs the same persisted bindings/contact context as runtime resolution. B04092 contact-target regression passes.
- Pick/skip/choice/optional carriers are marked as resumptions of the effect that was already resolving. They finish before triggers emitted by that effect's prefix and never enter the unresolved-effect ordering group.
- Direct invokeHiramekiOfCard/invokeLeaveToRemoveOfCard entries are new unresolved effects: they do not inherit a parent's confirmation, and retain invoked abilityId/description for the ordering UI.
- Real B03006 action-declare coverage proves a3/a4 are separate labeled entries and uses public reorder/confirm dispatch plus exact zone card IDs to prove execution order. Final Sol review and global docs/full gates remain before closure.

## Horizontal investigation

Review remediation: `EffectStackPanel` filters the priority owner before deriving its badge, rows, reorder controls, and confirmation IDs. It receives `pendingOwnerOrderGroup` from `Playmat` and preserves that canonical engine sequence; no UI timestamp sort can produce a rejected visible confirmation. Mixed-owner/cross-batch/legacy and timestamp-divergence regressions cover the snapshot contract.

Checked turn-player precedence, single-trigger resolution, cross-batch and legacy entries, AI/observer paths, UI reorder mutation, pending human choices, pick/skip/choice/optional continuation entries, deferred contact bindings, and nested hook emissions. The priority owner can order every eligible unresolved effect; non-turn player choices wait until turn-player entries finish. A paused current effect finishes before its newly emitted triggers. Nested emissions never inherit a parent's confirmation.

## Prevention

The UI must render the exact canonical engine entries it controls, filtering the owner before creating a confirmation snapshot.

Keep emission batch as provenance, not an ordering boundary. Test the public path with cross-batch and legacy entries: pending → reorder → explicit confirm → exact effect result. Include CPU-turn blocking, a confirmed parent that emits a new two-entry batch, and persisted contact bindings. UI must render the same GameState pending entries it controls.
