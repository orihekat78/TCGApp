## RCA

CutIn/Disguise, Misread, and Hirameki used inconsistent shells: scroll ownership and `min-block-size: 0` were missing, so short landscape height displaced the header or final action.

## Evidence

- `src/ui/components/CutInDisguisePickerModal.tsx`
- `src/ui/components/MisreadPickerModal.tsx`
- `src/ui/components/HiramekiPickerModal.tsx`
- `tests/e2e/bug-244-landscape-special-pickers.spec.ts`

## Verification

RED found CID pass below viewport, Misread y=-18.5, and Hirameki skip at 35px. GREEN runs 17 focused tests plus 3/3 mobile and desktop E2E flows with body-only scroll, 44px action, close, pending, and engine-result assertions. `c49850bb` hardens proof.

## Horizontal investigation

BUG-241's shared modal-stack shell was checked; only these three special pickers required the short-height remediation.

## Prevention

At formal mobile landscape 851x393, use flex-column with `overflow: hidden`, a `min-block-size: 0` scrolling body, and a fixed 44px final action. Portrait is out of scope.
