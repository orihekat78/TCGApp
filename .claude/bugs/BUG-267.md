## Expected

B10069 compares the source and target characters' effective AP at resolution.

## Actual

`apMax` accepted only a literal value, so source-dependent AP could not be
represented without a closure.

## RCA

Candidate matching intentionally lacks `EffectCtx`; the context-dependent
bound had to be resolved before candidate enumeration.

## Related files

- `src/engine/target/candidates.ts`
- `src/engine/read/char.ts`
- `src/engine/types/effect.ts`

## Horizontal investigation

`apMaxSource` is hoisted to literal `apMax` before matching and fails closed
without a source UID. Existing literal AP filters stay unchanged.

## Prevention

Use pre-enumeration context resolution for future dynamic numeric filters and
test an AP modifier on the source, the equality boundary, and missing source.
