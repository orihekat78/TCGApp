## RCA

The reveal timer continued behind a detail dialog, advancing the phase before the user could close that dialog.

## Evidence

- `src/ui/components/DeckRevealOverlay.tsx`
- `src/ui/components/CardExpandModal.tsx`
- `tests/e2e/card-choice-details-real-flow.spec.ts`

## Verification

The real-flow test opens detail during reveal, waits, closes it, and confirms the expected phase; follow-up `8a4462a7` hardens landscape decision flows.

## Horizontal investigation

Checked other timed overlays and all detail-opening choice paths for pause/resume ownership.

## Prevention

Timed presentation must pause while a higher-priority modal is open and resume only after its close transition.
