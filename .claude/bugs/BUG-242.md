## RCA

The hidden tile renderer omitted both the `CardArt(null)` fallback and an ordinal label, leaving a blank visual tile with an indistinguishable accessible name.

## Evidence

- `src/ui/components/SelectableCardTile.tsx`
- `src/ui/components/SetCardChoiceModalHost.tsx`
- `tests/ui/components/SelectableCardTile.test.tsx`
- `tests/e2e/set-card-choice.spec.ts`

## Verification

Focused tests cover loaded card backs, private metadata suppression, ordinal names, and instance callback identity; desktop/mobile Playwright resolves a real opaque B02039 choice.

## Horizontal investigation

EffectPicker hidden evidence, FileArea/EvidenceArea, face-up choices, and public detail labels were checked for privacy and identity boundaries.

## Prevention

Hidden selectable cards use `CardArt(null)`, `aria-hidden` artwork, a visible-safe ordinal label, and never expose a card ID, URL, or instance ID.
