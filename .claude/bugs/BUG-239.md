- `src/ui/components/DeckPlaceModalHost.tsx`

Pixel 5 landscape tests retain visible controls and exact engine payloads; `8a4462a7` adds fixed-rect and scroll proof.

Treat 851x393 as mobile landscape, not desktop; test visible footer controls, body-only scrolling, and resolution after detail close.
