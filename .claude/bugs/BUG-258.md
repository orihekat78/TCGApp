## Expected

B05080's optional hand removal and LP -1 resolves immediately after the
opponent's reasoning character sleeps, before mislead and evidence gain.

## Previous behavior

The card listens to `reasoning:end`. That hook occurs after mislead handling and
evidence addition, so its LP change cannot affect that reasoning's evidence.

## RCA

The card used the existing post-reasoning hook. The flow had no reaction window
that both preceded mislead/evidence and waited for optional human effects.

## Implementation

- Added `reasoning:after-sleep` and an engine-only continuation. Its GameState
  token is target-bound and single-use; no card DSL atom can invoke it. It waits
  for every reaction to settle, then runs mislead and evidence.
- B05080 keeps the reacting `uid`, owner-relative opponent matcher, optional
  discard chain, and LP -1 on that character.
- B01045, B03038, and B05019 also move to the earlier hook: all alter current
  reasoning evidence. B03038 suppresses the pending gain instead of undoing it.
