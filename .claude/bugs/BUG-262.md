## Expected

Cards selecting a card without an original ability use its printed ability
metadata. Disabling original text does not make it eligible.  A selector that
excludes CutIn and Hirameki rejects either keyword; scene grants are included
for that effective keyword check.

## Actual

`TargetFilter` has neither a declarative original-ability predicate nor a
keyword-exclusion predicate, so B10074 and B10102 cannot be implemented
without a closure or an incorrect state reader.

## Evidence

- Official CT-P10 Q&A identity: `f635e6f0e43b2694cd74d41a1c1794e16fc0773a`.
- Regression: `tests/engine/ctp10-original-ability-filter.test.ts` (RED).

## RCA

The existing `disabledOriginal` state correctly suppresses text execution, but
the shared target filters only expose effective properties and no printed-card
ability predicate.

## Fix plan

Add `TargetFilter.hasOriginalAbility` and `TargetFilter.keywordNot`. Evaluate
the former from registered CardDef metadata, and the latter through the
effective scene keyword reader or printed deck/bound definition.

## Horizontal investigation

The three independent filter evaluators are `matchOneFilter`,
`targetFilterToPredicateWithCtx`, and `boundMatchesFilter` in `cond/eval.ts`.
All must agree on static abilities and keyword scope.

## Prevention

Every new `TargetFilter` field must add an asymmetric test for target picks,
deck reveal, and bound conditions, plus the taskA whitelist synchronisation.
