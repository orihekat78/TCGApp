## Expected

A resolution prompt, resolver lock, or active ActionContext exclusively owns public dispatch. New turn actions wait; their normal turn/phase/owner checks still apply when unblocked.

## Actual

`isAllowed` checked each action's local flow predicate only. The underlying declared-ability gate also omitted current-turn ownership and main-phase admission, so a no-cost ability could begin from an invalid timing or while a pending prompt, lock, or active action context was unresolved.

## RCA

Dispatcher admission had no shared boundary between primary turn actions and continuation/resolution actions. The engine declaration predicate was structural only, so UI, AI, direct engine, and public dispatch could diverge.

## Fix

`canDeclaredAbility` remains structural. `canActivateDeclaredAbility` owns current-player/main-phase and engine resolution admission; activation, UI, AI, and dispatcher share it. `can-check.ts` additionally rejects every fresh primary action while UI-owned resolution state exists. Resolver actions remain individually gated by their own pending witness.

## Horizontal investigation

Covered reasoning, hand use, hint, partner/declared abilities, assist/solve, action declaration, and end turn as primary actions. Existing flow predicates retain turn, phase, and owner validation.

## Prevention

Keep cross-cutting public dispatch ownership in the shared admission gate. Add a regression whenever a new primary `EngineAction` is introduced.
