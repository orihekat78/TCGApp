## Expected

`forEach` pauses after a body creates a pending pick.  After that pick and its
inner continuation resolve, it runs the next target against the updated state.

## Actual

The resolver starts every remaining iteration immediately.  Later picks see
the pre-pick deck and can repeat the same card.

## Evidence

- Official CT-P10 B10102 Q&A requires a one-card deck look for each eligible
  character, in order.
- Regression: `tests/cards/ct-p10/original-ability-filter.test.ts` (RED).

## RCA

`sequence` and `chain` attach their tail to the first new pending pick.
`forEach` only has an equivalent pause for deck-bottom ordering, not effect
picks.

## Related files

- `src/engine/effect/resolver.ts`
- `src/engine/effect/apply-pick.ts`
- `tests/cards/ct-p10/original-ability-filter.test.ts`

## Horizontal investigation

Check nested continuations, zero remaining targets, deck reorder pauses, AI
pick draining, and `fromBound` cleanup alongside the new pick pause.

## Prevention

Every looping resolver form that can execute an interactive body must stop at
the first pending decision and resume only through its continuation chain.
