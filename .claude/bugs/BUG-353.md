# BUG-353: stacked-card identities were incorrectly hidden

## Status

Fixed in QA Wave88.

## Authority

- B08003 official Q&A permits both players to inspect physical cards stacked
  under a character at any time.
- The cards remain informationless for rules evaluation: names, colors,
  traits, abilities, and MR behavior are not active while stacked.

## Defect

- Generic and B08003 cost pickers replaced exact IDs with card backs.
- Pending save state and replay projection redacted modern exact identities.
- The scene badge showed only a count and had no inspection interaction.

This prevented informed B06005 and B08003 choices.

## Fix

- Surface modern exact stack identities through pending/save, cost, scene
  inspection, and replay projection.
- Keep legacy count-only placeholder identities hidden.
- Add a keyboard-accessible stack badge above the scene-pick overlay.
- Keep all engine rules readers informationless.
