# Opaque React namespace mutation

## Expected

Only the integrity-pinned vendor interop result may be trusted as React, and no
opaque mutation may replace its `useCallback` export.

## Actual

Computed writes, nonliteral bulk mutations, and a spoofed interop argument shape
could preserve trusted React namespace status.

## RCA

Trust revocation covered explicit `useCallback` keys only. Interop provenance
accepted the vendor factory call in any argument position.

## Fix

Revoke trust for opaque computed or bulk mutations and require the exact pinned
interop call shape `interop(vendorFactory(), 1)`.

## Prevention

Mutation matrices and a trusted-import provenance helper cover source-shape and
spoofing regressions independently of generated chunk names.
