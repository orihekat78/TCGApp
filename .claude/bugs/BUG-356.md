# BUG-356: D11005 used a fixed AP removal threshold

## Expected

D11005/D11006 compare each target against the source character's effective AP
when their entry effect resolves.

## Actual

- The target filter hardcoded 8000.
- AP modifiers could not lower or raise the legal target boundary.
- The custom closure also made a surfaced public pick non-serializable.

## Root cause

The card predated the shipped `apMaxSource` target primitive and retained a
temporary custom closure despite the engine gaining source-aware candidates.

## Fix

Replace the closure with the serializable shipped filter
`{ kind: 'character', apMaxSource: true }`. No engine extension is required.

## Horizontal investigation

- The engine primitive already includes turn and continuous AP modifiers and
  fails closed without a source UID.
- D11005/D11006 were the remaining shipped custom approximation for this text.
