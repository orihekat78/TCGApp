## Expected

B03020's optional effect needs three deck cards. With fewer than three, it reveals nothing and resolves none of its later AP or removal clauses. With exactly three, it resolves normally and refresh occurs only after the revealed cards leave the deck.

## Actual

The shared `deckRevealUntil(maxN:3)` correctly implements ordinary look-at-available semantics with `min(deck.length, 3)`. B03020 inherited that behavior, so one or two cards were revealed and its later clauses could resolve.

## RCA

This Q&A is an exact-count all-or-nothing gate, whereas the shared primitive intentionally supports cards that inspect every remaining card. B03020 had no descriptor-level gate around its sequence.

- `src/cards/ct-p03/B03020.ts`

## Fix

Wrap B03020's inner sequence in `conditional{if:deckAtLeast(self,3)}`. The serializable reader checks the source player's deck before any reveal, including after save/replay JSON round-trips. No shared reveal behavior changes.

## Horizontal investigation

`deckRevealUntil` is also used by cards whose Q&A requires revealing all remaining cards when fewer than N exist; changing its shared `min(deck.length, maxN)` behavior would regress them. The gate therefore belongs only on B03020.

## Prevention

Keep public action-flow cases for deck 0/1/2/3, match/no-match, optional decline, and the exact-three refresh boundary. Treat exact-N Q&A as an explicit card-level gate unless the official text establishes a shared rule.

## Ruling note

The official material reviewed here does not settle whether the three-card count is fixed at trigger time or checked when the optional effect resolves. The engine currently checks at resolution time. A regression test pins both deck-gained and deck-lost cases without presenting that policy as an official ruling.
