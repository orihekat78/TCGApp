## Expected

A printed continuous effect saying a character cannot reason prevents that
bearer from reasoning while it is active, and disabling original abilities
restores the normal rule.

## Actual

`canReason` only reads the temporary `turnEffects.cannotReason` marker.  It
does not read an active continuous modifier, so B10079 cannot be represented.

## RCA

The existing bearer-only boolean reader supports action, guard, and CutIn
prohibitions but omitted the matching reasoning token.

## Related files

- `src/engine/types/card-def.ts`
- `src/engine/read/char.ts`
- `src/engine/flow/main/reasoning.ts`

## Horizontal investigation

The reader must preserve temporary reason bans, disabled-original behavior,
and must not affect partners or unrelated action/guard readers.

## Prevention

New bearer-only continuous prohibitions must be routed through the shared
reader and their single flow gate, with a disabled-original regression.
