## Expected

【宣言】 is unavailable unless its full cost is payable, or an eligible alternative-cost provider is available. This must hold at the engine authorization and dispatch boundary, not only in UI enumeration.

## Actual

The activation path combined `canDeclaredAbility` with payment but had no shared cost-aware authorization gate. A direct `declaredAbility` dispatch could call payment with an empty or insufficient hand; `removeFromHand` then continued and resolved the effect.

## RCA

Cost checks were duplicated in UI/AI enumerators but absent from the shared activation gate used by `activateDeclaredAbility` and `useEngineDispatch`. Initial remediation also evaluated composite items independently against one pre-payment state and did not validate every explicit cost witness.

## Horizontal investigation

Checked scene/case/hand/partner-MR UI enumerators, scene/case/partner-MR AI enumeration, alternative-cost providers, nested/shared-resource composites, and all explicit cost fields: evidence indices, scene UIDs, remove/partner-area IDs, set-card hosts, and stacked instance IDs. On-hand AI enumeration is tracked separately as BUG-246. Generated probes now seed numeric `n` recursively and assert partner removal and scene-to-stack consumption.

## Prevention

Keep cost-aware authorization in `canActivateDeclaredAbility`; use a pure sequential transition simulator for composite payment and explicit witnesses. Never call custom cost closures during authorization. `canDeclaredAbility` stays structural. Generate cost-valid, branch-specific happy-path probes from freshly dumped shipped DSL before asserting effects and cost consumption. Regression coverage uses the public dispatcher plus scene UI/AI parity.
