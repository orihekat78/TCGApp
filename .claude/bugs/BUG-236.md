## RCA

The detail dialog remained inside local overlay stacking contexts, allowing a decision overlay or HUD to win pointer hit testing.

## Evidence

- `src/ui/components/CardExpandModal.tsx`
- `src/ui/components/CardExpandModal.css`
- `tests/e2e/card-choice-details-real-flow.spec.ts`
- `tests/e2e/user-bug-wave-ui.spec.ts`

## Verification

Desktop and mobile real-flow tests open, close, and resume details; follow-up `c4d0bb77` closes the analogous CardList/HUD path.

## Horizontal investigation

Reviewed CardList, effect, guard, toast, HUD, and deck-reveal overlays for stacking and pointer ownership.

## Prevention

Portal global detail UI above decision layers and assert real clicks, never forced clicks, at the formal mobile viewport.
