# Comma Reflect timer invocation

## Expected

String timer handlers are rejected when `Reflect.apply` is selected through a
comma expression, including a browser-qualified namespace.

## Actual

Invocation normalization handled parentheses, aliases, members, call, and bind,
but not the value-producing right side of a comma expression.

## Related files

- `scripts/private-hosted/audit-runtime-boundary.ts`
- `tests/release/private-hosted-runtime-boundary.test.ts`

## RCA and fix

The callee resolver stopped at `BinaryExpression`. Normalize comma callees to
their right-hand value before timer and Reflect provenance resolution.

## Horizontal investigation and prevention

Source and bundle regressions cover bare and qualified comma forms alongside
direct, aliased, projected, call, and bind forms.
